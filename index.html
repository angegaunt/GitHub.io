About

Editorial craft, strategic structure.

I'm a content director with 15+ years leading editorial and content strategy for health care, senior care, and mission-driven organizations — Mayo Clinic, the American Academy of Family Physicians, the PKD Foundation, and currently A Place for Mom, where I lead the strategy behind the company's organic and AI search visibility.

My work sits at the intersection of editorial craft and structured strategy: translating proprietary research, clinical nuance, and customer outcomes into content that people trust — and that search engines, and now AI answer engines, trust too.

Health content earns trust one accurate, well-edited sentence at a time.

Along the way I've built editorial governance models and content roadmaps, managed freelance writers and agency partners, and helped organizations responsibly adopt AI-assisted content workflows without losing the human judgment that makes health content credible.

Outside of work, I'm usually outdoors with my family and our dogs, on the racquetball court, cooking, or a few chapters into a mystery novel.
