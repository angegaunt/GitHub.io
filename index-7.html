About

Content strategy is at the core of what I do.

I'm a content and editorial leader with more than 15 years of experience helping brands build authority, grow audiences, and drive measurable business results. My expertise spans content strategy, SEO, AI search (GEO/AEO), editorial operations, and content governance. I specialize in health care, and I particularly enjoy translating complex topics into clear, engaging experiences that inform, inspire, and create lasting value.

Throughout my career, I've partnered with organizations including A Place for Mom, Mayo Clinic, the American Academy of Family Physicians, and the PKD Foundation to create digital experiences that build trust and drive growth.

My work sits at the intersection of editorial craft and structured strategy: translating clinical nuance, proprietary research, and brand positioning into multimodal content that speaks credibly to diverse audiences, having developed successful strategies to reach consumers, patients, physicians, donors, prospects, and members, while holding up to legal, clinical, and brand review along the way.

I love writing and editing just as much as I love strategizing.

From launching thought leadership programs to scaling editorial operations and implementing AI-enabled workflows, I'm passionate about developing systems that help teams produce exceptional content more efficiently and effectively, without losing the human judgment that makes regulated content credible.

Outside of work, I'm usually outdoors with my family and our dogs, on the racquetball court, cooking, or a few chapters into a mystery novel.
